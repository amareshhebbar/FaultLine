## What is Faultline?

Car makers crash their own cars into walls on purpose. Because they caused the crash, they know exactly what happened, and they can check whether the airbags worked.

Faultline does this for software. We build a small but real online shop backend, break it on purpose in many ways, and build an AI detective that reads the logs, metrics and traces and explains **what broke, where, and why**, with proof attached to every claim.

Because we caused every incident, we already know the right answer. That lets us give the detective a real score.

## Why it is different

Most AI incident tools cannot tell you how often they are right. Faultline creates its own labelled dataset automatically, one incident at a time, so every result comes with an accuracy number behind it.

<Note>
The end goal is a sentence like: "Root cause identified correctly in X% of 500 injected incidents, with zero uncited claims." The X is only written down after it is measured.
</Note>
